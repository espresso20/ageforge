# Resources

AgeForge has 21 resources that unlock progressively as you advance through the ages. Resources accumulate passively based on your buildings and villager assignments.

## Food

Food is the most critical resource. It feeds your population at a rate of **0.5 food/tick per villager**. If food hits zero, population begins to starve and you may lose villagers.

**How to increase food:**
- Assign workers to food gathering (`assign worker food 3`)
- Build Gathering Camps, Farms, Plantations
- Research Agriculture (+50% food production)
- Research Colonialism (+200% food production)

**Watch the food rate** in the Economy tab — the `+N/t` shown after the bar. Keep it positive.

---

## Knowledge

Knowledge is the lifeblood of your civilization's progress. All technologies cost knowledge and all research requires it to accumulate.

**How to increase knowledge:**
- Assign shamans (`assign shaman knowledge 1`)
- Build Altars, Libraries, Universities, Observatories
- Research Primitive Writing, Philosophy, Printing Press, Computers

Knowledge storage starts at 30 — build Libraries early to raise the cap.

---

## Gold

Gold enables trade, diplomacy gifts, and many mid-to-late game building costs.

**How to increase gold:**
- Build Markets, Banks, Stock Exchanges
- Complete trade routes that export surplus resources
- Research Currency, Mercantilism, Mass Production, Social Media

---

## Late-game resources

Late resources (Uranium, Plasma, Titanium, Dark Matter, Antimatter, Quantum Flux) have very low base storage and require dedicated infrastructure. Prioritise storage buildings before trying to accumulate them.

| Resource | Primary buildings |
|---|---|
| Uranium | Nuclear Plant, Reactor |
| Plasma | Fusion Reactor, Stellar Cradle |
| Titanium | Orbital Platform, Space Station |
| Dark Matter | Deep Space Array, Warp Nexus |
| Quantum Flux | Reality Engine, Reality Anchor |
