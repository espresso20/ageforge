# Milestones & Chains

33 milestones are grouped into 5 chains. Completing all milestones in a chain grants your civilization a **title** and a **temporary speed boost**. Some milestones are hidden until you make progress toward them.

---

## Civilization titles

When a chain is completed, its title overrides any count-based fallback:

| Chain | Title | Speed Boost |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | "The Founders" | +3.0× for 180 ticks |
| Scholar | "The Enlightened" | +3.0× for 180 ticks |
| Builder | "The Architects" | +1.5× for 90 ticks |
| Military | "The Conquerors" | +0.5× for 30 ticks |
| Ancient Ages | (per age reached) | varies |

If no chain is completed, fallback titles apply:

| Milestones completed | Title |
|---|---|
| 3+ | Aspiring |
| 8+ | Rising Power |
| 15+ | Established |
| 22+ | Dominant Force |
| 30+ | Legend |

## Tips

- **Prioritise Scholar and Settlement chains** — their speed boosts are enormous (+3.0× for 3 minutes) and the milestones align naturally with good play
- **Hidden milestones** appear in the Milestones panel once you have >50% progress toward them or have completed the preceding age
- Chain speed boosts stack with milestone bonuses and prestige upgrades — when multiple boosts fire at once, the multiplication is dramatic
- The **Transcended** milestone completing the Ancient Ages chain is effectively the game's victory condition before prestige
