# Badges

A badge is a permanent mark on your account for something you did in a game. There are 242 badges to earn, in 12 families: one for every age, wonder, harbinger and awakening, one for each era's catastrophe and expedition kinds, and a set of one-off feats. Alongside them, counts kept across all your runs are not badges but tallies: one coin each that is struck finer as the count climbs. A badge is earned once and kept for good, through every prestige and every new game.

Badges are cosmetic. A badge gives points, sometimes a title or a theme, and nothing inside a run: the same game plays the same way whatever the account has earned. They are separate from [milestones](milestones.md), which belong to one run, pay a reward inside it and start over with the next.

## Seeing your badges

Open the coin cabinet to look through them:

```
badges
```

<figure class="screen" data-screen="cabinet"><figcaption>The coin cabinet as it opens: a drawer for each age, then Ledgers, Curios and Themes. The plate at the top counts the pieces held and the sets finished.</figcaption></figure>

[Accounts & Recovery](account.md#the-coin-cabinet) describes the cabinet itself: the drawers, the wells, the keys and the mouse. The Stats panel's Lifetime part shows how many you have earned. `account badges` prints a text list instead: your count, points and title, then each family you have something in sight of, with its earned badges, the first four it has still to earn and how many secrets it keeps.

## Coins, levels and stacks

Every badge you earn is kept as a coin, and the cabinet shows the coins, not a list. The cabinet has 25 drawers: one for each of the 22 ages, then Ledgers, Curios and Themes. Each age's drawer has six wells: one for each kind of coin the age has, and a larger one in the middle for its legendary.

| Well | What strikes its coin |
|---|---|
| Age | Reaching the age |
| Wonder | Raising the age's wonder |
| Techs | Researching every tech of the age in one run |
| Maximalist | The age's Maximalist badge |
| Harbinger | Meeting the age's harbinger |
| Legendary | The age's one hard feat |

Each age's money is its own: the coins of one age differ from another's in shape, metal and make, and the five kinds within an age are different pieces of that age's money, not one coin with five signs.

**Six levels, by how soon in the age.** A coin is struck at a level from 1 to 6, and the level depends on how soon in the age it was earned, measured against the age's pacing target (the time a first run is expected to spend there, which shortens with the game's speed). The same marks hold in every age:

| Level | Struck within |
|---|---|
| Level 6 | 60% of the age's target time |
| Level 5 | 80% |
| Level 4 | 100% |
| Level 3 | 130% |
| Level 2 | 180% |
| Level 1 | Later than that |

Luck plays no part: the marks come from the pacing model and the time spent in the age.

**One copy per run, stacked.** A run that earns a coin adds a copy to its well. Two runs make two copies, and the copies stack in the well, the finest on top. Earning the same coin again in the same run only improves that run's copy, if it comes at a finer level. Select a well and press `Enter` to fan the stack out and pick one copy. No two copies are alike: each has its own seed, so its outline, how far off-center the strike sits and where the wear falls are its own.

**One legendary feat per age.** Each age has one hard feat that no other coin asks for. Striking it puts a legendary coin in the age's middle well: a larger, still coin with no level of its own, above level 6. There are 22 of them, one for each age.

**Tallies as one improving coin.** A count that climbs across all your runs does not make a coin for every rung. It keeps one coin, which is struck again one level finer at each rung. See [Tallies](#tallies).

## How a badge is earned

Every badge is one of three kinds, and its description says which:

| Kind | Earned when | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A moment | Something happens, once | Reach the Stone Age |
| In one run | A count or a condition inside a single run is met. It starts over with the next run | Take 10 deals from one civilization in one run |
| Across all your runs | A count kept on the account reaches its mark. It never goes down, and a prestige does not reset it | Prestige 10 times |

A few rules hold for all of them:

- A badge goes to the account the game belongs to. A game started or loaded under one account never earns for another.
- Building counts ignore selling and rebuilding. A copy counts only when it takes that building past the most the run has built of it, and an upgrade is not a built copy.
- Production counts what was made. A resource tally counts what your buildings, workers and techs produce. Trades, loot, refunds and gifts are not production.
- Time away counts. What the game produced while you were gone counts the same as what it produced while you watched.
- A modified game marks what it earns. A badge earned in a save that was edited outside the game is listed as earned in a modified game and adds no points.
- The developer console does not block badges. A game it has changed still earns like any other.

## Tiers, rarity and points

Each badge has a tier, and a tier is worth points. Your points add up to a score.

| Tier | Points | Usual rarity |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 5 | Common |
| Silver | 10 | Uncommon |
| Gold | 25 | Rare |
| Platinum | 50 | Epic |
| Legendary | 100 | Legendary |

A badge's rarity follows its tier unless the badge says otherwise. The 22 Maximalists are gold but epic, and two of the specials are rarer than their tier: one gold badge is epic and one platinum badge is legendary. Every badge together is worth 4,430 points. A tally's coin is worth none: the cabinet shows it, but it adds nothing to the score.

## The families

| Family | Badges | What they are for |
|---|---|---|
| **Ages** | 21 | Reaching each age after the first |
| **Swift Eras** | 7 | Leaving each of the seven eras within its pacing target |
| **Wonders** | 22 | Raising each age's wonder |
| **Maximalists** | 22 | Having a set number of an age's buildings built at the same time, before you leave the age. Wonders do not count |
| **Research** | 22 | Researching every tech of an age in one run |
| **Catastrophes** | 15 | Enduring and succumbing to each era's catastrophe, and the Last Passage |
| **Expeditions** | 21 | Coming back from a successful scout, long or grand mission in each era |
| **Harbingers** | 22 | Meeting each of the 22 harbingers |
| **Awakenings** | 7 | Witnessing each awakening |
| **Themes** | 16 | Advancing an age while wearing each theme |
| **Maps** | 2 | Advancing an age with each glyph set |
| **Specials** | 65 | One-off feats, from the easy to the very hard |

One special asks you to save up. Everything Full is earned in the Industrial Age or later by holding at least 6 hours of your income in every resource you can save up, all at once. Your income is what your town makes of each resource at that moment, so it rises as you grow and the bar rises with it. Food, faith, culture and nanobots are spent as they are made and do not count, and neither does a resource with no income.

Three more badges sit outside the families. They are worth no points, count toward nothing, and are not listed until they are earned.

## Tallies

A tally is a count that climbs across all your runs and keeps one coin. The coin is struck at level 1 when the count reaches the first rung and one level finer at each rung after, up to level 6. A tally with fewer than six rungs skips levels, so its last rung is always level 6: the rung's place in the climb is spread over the six levels, halves up. A level never goes down. A tally's coin is not a badge: it has no tier, no points and no rarity.

The cabinet keeps tallies in drawers that belong to no age. The Ledgers drawer holds the 74 tallies below, the Curios drawer the one-off feats and the other badges that belong to no age, and the Themes drawer the themes. Each opens like an age's drawer, with a well for each coin, and pages through them with `PgUp` and `PgDn` when they do not all fit.

When you load an account that has already passed a rung its coin does not show, the game strikes the coin to the level it has earned, without a fanfare. A coin never goes down.

### Resource tallies

Each resource has a tally of up to four rungs. The first rung is what an ordinary town makes of the resource in the first age it makes any, so a first run earns it in that age or soon after. The top rung is 25 full runs of it, and the rungs between climb in even steps, each the same number of times the one before. A resource that only comes after a first run's last age has no Trickle, and its top rung is measured over its own first two ages. Soldiers have a tally too, and it counts the soldiers who take their posts. Each has its coin in the Ledgers drawer.

Three of them, as examples:

| Food stores | 4.3K | 1.4M | 510M | 170B |
| Wood stores | 2.2K | 380K | 67M | 11B |
| Stone stores | 44K | 44M | 45B | 46T |

## What stays hidden

The cabinet never names something the game has not shown you yet:

- A badge for reaching an age shows once that age is your next age, because the long bar in the header already names it. Badges tied to an age (its wonder, its Maximalist and Research badges, and the lineage, resource and payroll tallies that begin in it) stay hidden until you reach that age.
- A badge about a civilization or a harbinger stays hidden until you have met it, one about an awakening until it happens, and one about a catastrophe until its warning names it or it strikes.
- The badge for wearing a theme that a badge unlocks stays hidden until you hold the theme.
- A family with nothing in sight has no tab and no heading until you have reached the last age.
- The counts cover what you can see. `account badges` starts with a line such as `3 of 127 earned, 15 points, ??? hidden`. The game does not say how many badges are hidden until an account has reached the last age.

**11 badges are secret.** A secret badge shows a one-line hint in the cabinet and nothing else, and it keeps its name until you earn it. This page counts them and does not name them. The three badges outside the families are not listed at all until they are earned.

## Titles

Your score holds a title. Every account starts as a Settler.

| Title | Score |
|---|---|
| Settler | 0 |
| Headman | 250 |
| Magistrate | 1,000 |
| Sovereign | 3,000 |
| Paragon | 6,000 |
| Eternal | 10,000 |
| Completionist | Every badge that counts, none of them earned in a modified game |

Six more titles come with badges, not with the score:

| Title | Comes with |
|---|---|
| Survivor | The third rung of Dooms endured |
| Merchant Prince | The top rung of Faction deals |
| Harbinger Whisperer | The top rung of Harbingers heeded |
| Cosmic Heir | Succumbing to the Last Passage |
| The Undying | Enduring every catastrophe and the Last Passage |
| Hut Magnate | A secret badge |

You wear one title at a time. Once you wear anything but Settler and the window has room, it shows beside your account's name in the header's top edge.

```
title
title survivor
title default
```

Bare `title` lists the titles you hold and marks the one you wear. `title <name>` wears one you hold, and the start of the name is enough. `title default` goes back to the title your score holds. A badge earned in a modified game gives no title. A title changes nothing in a game.

## Themes from badges

Ten [themes](themes.md) are the reward of a badge. Earning the badge unlocks the theme on the account, for good, and the log says so.

| Theme | Comes with |
|---|---|
| Bronze | Reaching the Bronze Age |
| Parchment | Reaching the Renaissance Age |
| Monochrome | Reaching the Information Age |
| Cyberpunk | Reaching the Cyberpunk Age |
| Cosmic | Reaching the Galactic Age |
| Ledger | The top rung of Faction deals (100 deals) |
| Ashfall | Succumbing to every era's catastrophe |
| Prismatic | Earning 160 badges |
| Source | A secret badge |
| Glitch | A secret badge |

The five age themes come with the age badges. If an account reached one of those ages before it held the badge, the game adds the badge and the theme when it loads the account.

## Where badges are stored

Badges are kept in `badges.json`, beside `account.json` in the account's slot (`data/accounts/<id>/`): the badges you have earned, the counts behind the ones that climb across runs, and the days you have played. The title you chose to wear is a preference, so it lives in `settings.json` in the same slot.

An [export](account.md#exporting-amp-importing-accounts) carries your badges and a [backup](account.md#backups) copies them. [Where badges are stored](account.md#where-badges-are-stored) has the details, including what happens to an edited file.
