AgeForge

A civilization for your terminal

AgeForge is an idle game with a progression-style game plan where you build a civilization. Build, explore, conquer, and advance your civilization to the end of ages.

The Compendium

To get a copy

curl -fLo ageforge https://github.com/espresso20/ageforge/releases/latest/download/ageforge-macos-arm64 && chmod +x ageforge && ./ageforge
For Apple Silicon. On an Intel Mac, use ageforge-macos-amd64 instead.
